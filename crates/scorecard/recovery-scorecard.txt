Reference recovery scorecard
============================

Verbatim output of `cargo run --release -p sumi-scorecard`.
Everything the harness measures is seeded and
count-based — no timing — so this file must reproduce byte for byte on any
machine; regenerate it whenever recovery behavior legitimately changes, and
treat any other drift as a regression.

Reading the numbers:
- Part A preservation of 1.0 across all eight classes at 10^4 pairs each
  extends the recovery property tests' guarantee (256 cases per CI run) by
  two orders of magnitude: no single-token edit has ever disturbed an
  untouched top-level item. Its diagnostic counts are the frontend's;
  type-dependent unused-value errors belong to semantic checking and are
  not included.
- Part B partner churn tops out at the bracket nesting depth around the
  edit (max 8 tokens of ~227k), boundary churn at 1, items disturbed at 0:
  one unmatched opener or deleted closer does not re-pair the rest of the
  file. Nearest-match pairing strands the damaged opener instead of
  cascading, and a closer can only pair across an item boundary when two
  complementary errors sit in different items — which no single edit
  creates.
- Part C spread stays inside the line for both edits: a deleted quote
  reaches a median of a few dozen bytes and a maximum of one long line,
  and disturbs no item. With no multi-line literal form, no delimiter can
  re-pair anything past its line.
